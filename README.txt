#sds192project-2
Authors: Amelia Dion, Blair Pasarkar, Aria Souza Asevedo, Cassia Lee
This is our Project 2: NYC Film Permits. 
Our project's purpose was to explore the NYC Open Film Permit Data and want to 
see which Borough is being allocated the most film permits and in what 
circumstances those permits are being allocated. With that we are analyzing 
seasonal trends and differences across boroughs. Our main goal is to see how 
different variables such as Borough, Category, StartDateTime
(mutated into Seasons),EventType, and SubCategoryName impact film permit usage
in New York City. The data is provided by the Mayor's Office of Media and 
Entertainment(MOME) and the owner of the data set is NYC OpenData. The qmd files 
hold our data tidying, ggplots related to EDA, and ggplots related to overall
analysis.
